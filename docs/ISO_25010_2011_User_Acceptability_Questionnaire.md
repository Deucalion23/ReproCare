# USER ACCEPTABILITY QUESTIONNAIRE

## ReproCare: Maternal and Reproductive Health Tracking and Learning System

**For Rural Health Unit 1, San Carlos City, Pangasinan**

### Purpose

This questionnaire measures the acceptability of ReproCare using the ISO/IEC 25010:2011 software product quality model. It supports the study objectives of improving maternal and reproductive health record management, monitoring, health education, and AI-assisted decision support.

Your responses will be kept confidential and will be used only for this academic study. Do not write your name unless you choose to do so.

### Researcher note: scoring and analysis

- Calculate the weighted mean per item, subcharacteristic, characteristic, and overall end-user acceptability score.
- Exclude `N/A` answers from the denominator for the relevant item.
- Analyze Part II independently; do not combine technical-maintainability ratings with end-user ratings.
- Suggested verbal interpretation: 4.21-5.00 = Highly Acceptable; 3.41-4.20 = Acceptable; 2.61-3.40 = Moderately Acceptable; 1.81-2.60 = Less Acceptable; 1.00-1.80 = Not Acceptable.
- The AI item evaluates usefulness and clarity of the feature, not whether it replaces clinical judgment. Clinical decisions remain the responsibility of qualified healthcare professionals.
